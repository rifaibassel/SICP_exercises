We cannot use 'fast-expt' as Alyssa suggests as it will make our process way slower. This is due to the fact that fast-expt will result in the interpreter calculating the large number (base^exponent) and then finding its remainder while the expmod procedure keeps taking the remainder at each step providing an upper bound for what the interpreter will calculate (which is the number we are dividing by - 1 (or m - 1 in the expmod procedure))
